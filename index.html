About

I am a Research Associate at the University of Bristol, working in soft robotics and biomedical engineering. My research spans hydrogels, responsive elastomers and biohybrid systems, exploring how material design and fabrication can enable soft actuation and biomedical applications.

SoftBioX brings together my research interests and publications across these areas.

Research

Highly Entangled Hydrogels

Hydrogel design and fabrication for soft hydraulic actuation.

Photoresponsive PU Elastomers

Light-responsive materials and biohybrid muscle actuation.

Interpenetrating Elastomer Networks

Programmable soft materials for paediatric airway stents.

Adaptive Bio-interfaces

Scaffolds and material platforms for tissue engineering.
