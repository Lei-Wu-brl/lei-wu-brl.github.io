About

Soft-matter mechanics for robotic and biomedical systems

I am a Research Associate in the School of Engineering Mathematics and Technology at the University of Bristol, based in SoftLab at the Bristol Robotics Laboratory and working with Professor Jonathan Rossiter.

My research combines solid mechanics, polymer chemistry, robotics and biomedical engineering. My early work focused on hydrogel composites and dielectric elastomers. During my PhD at UCL, I developed photoresponsive polyurethane systems and nanofibre scaffolds for soft and biohybrid actuation. Subsequent medical-engineering research connected these material capabilities with soft-device fabrication and evaluation.

At Bristol, I develop highly entangled hydrogels, artificial muscles and tissue-mimetic systems. I investigate how composition, architecture and interfaces determine mechanical behaviour, and how those relationships can guide reliable actuation and biomedical device design.

SoftBioX is my personal research website, bringing together my research interests, publications and academic background.

Research

Three connected research directions

My approach links a physical question to a material or structural strategy, then tests the connection through experiments and device demonstrations.

01 · Materials & mechanics

Soft materials and interfaces

How can soft materials sustain large deformation while retaining strength and reliable interfaces?

I study hydrogels, elastomers and their composites, connecting material architecture with mechanical behaviour, bonding and cyclic durability.

02 · Actuation

Soft and biohybrid robotics

How can material response and living muscle generate controlled motion?

My work explores soft hydraulic actuators, photoresponsive polymers and engineered skeletal muscle systems, linking structure and stimulus to actuation.

03 · Biomedical engineering

Devices and tissue interaction

How can soft systems reproduce or interact with the mechanics of biological tissues?

I work on compliant medical devices and tissue models, with an interest in quantifying the relationship between material properties, anatomical structure and device performance.

Printed hydrogels for hydraulic actuation

Using highly entangled hydrogels and 3D printing to develop soft hydraulic actuators, connecting material mechanics with force generation and response.

My contribution. First and co-corresponding author. I led actuator fabrication, mechanical and hydraulic testing, data analysis and manuscript preparation.

Biohybrids · Interface Focus 2026

Responsive fibres and living muscle

Combining photoresponsive elastomer nanofibres with skeletal muscle to investigate how an engineered material environment can support biohybrid actuation.

My contribution. First author. I led responsive nanofibre scaffold development and characterisation, coordinated integration with engineered myofibres, and prepared the manuscript.

Medical devices · Advanced Functional Materials

Programmable materials for a laryngeal stent

Developing an interpenetrating elastomer network composite for a bespoke paediatric laryngeal stent, linking material design with a specific reconstructive need.

My contribution. Co-first author. I contributed composite-material design, fabrication, mechanical characterisation and manuscript development.

Soft robotic support and simulation

A soft robotic concept and simulator for studying female stress urinary incontinence, using a controlled experimental platform to investigate device function.
