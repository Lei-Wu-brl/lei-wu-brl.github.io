SoftBioX Lab

Crossing Soft Robotics and Biomedical Engineering.

We explore the interface between soft robotics, biomaterials, and medical engineering to create adaptive systems for healthcare.

About

SoftBioX Lab is dedicated to advancing soft robotic systems and bio-materials for clinical translation. We develop adaptive material–structure platforms using novel fabrication techniques such as hydrogel entanglement, 3D inverse printing, and photoresponsive elastomers.

Research

Highly Entangled Hydrogels

Soft actuators & GI tract phantoms.

Photoresponsive PU Elastomers

Light-driven contractile systems.

Interpenetrating Elastomer Networks

Paediatric airway stents & EB wound dressings.

Adaptive Bio-interfaces

Materials–tissue interaction platforms.
